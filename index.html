Frequently Asked Questions

Important information about the registration process.

1. Who can register?

Students in Grade 9 and Grade 10 can use this registration form.

2. How much is the registration fee?

The registration fee is 50 Ethiopian Birr (ETB).

3. How do I send my registration details?

Complete the form and select an admin. The website will prepare your message. Copy it, open Telegram, find the selected admin, paste the message, and send it.

4. Will my payment screenshot be attached automatically?

No. Open your Telegram conversation and attach the payment screenshot manually if required by the admin. Then send the message.

5. Does completing the form confirm my registration?

No. Preparing the message does not confirm registration or verify payment. Follow up with your selected Telegram admin for confirmation.

6. When is the deadline?

The deadline is November 24, 2026, at 12:30 PM Ethiopia time (UTC+3). The form closes when the deadline passes.
